Flycast WASM and EmulatorJS

The game runs through the GPLv2-licensed Flycast WASM port (Flycast upstream plus browser SH4-to-WASM JIT by Nick Somers), presented through EmulatorJS. The embedded player is the browser launcher linked by the game's official repository; its Flycast build identifies itself as flycast-wasm-v1.0-homebrew.1-2026-09-01. This wrapper does not modify or redistribute the emulator core.
